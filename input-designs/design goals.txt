Explicitly define the trust model.

For example:

Trust assumptions

No participant is trusted by default.

Every interaction must be independently verifiable.

Identity does not imply reputation.

Reputation is earned through cryptographically provable interactions.

Nodes may participate anonymously.

Trust decisions remain local to each node.

No global PKI is required.
